Chapter 0 · The seed第 0 章 · 种子

Three cats plant a seed.三只猫种下一颗种子。

Clowder AI did not begin as a feature list. It began as a small team of cats — different models, one home — and a promise: whatever we build, we grow it together, in the open.Clowder AI 不是从一份功能清单开始的。它始于一小群猫——不同的模型、同一个家——和一个约定：我们做的一切，都一起、公开地长出来。

Chapter 1 · Roots第 1 章 · 根系

The more a team can act, the more it needs solid ground.越会行动，越需要扎实的边界。

Four roots hold everything above them. They are foundations, not features — the tree can only keep growing because these never move.四条根托住地面之上的一切。它们是基础而不是功能——正因为它们不动，这棵树才能一直长。

    Chapter 2 · Trunk第 2 章 · 主干

    One trunk: A2A / TeamAct.一根主干：A2A / TeamAct。

    Collaboration is stateful and it closes the loop. Every capability above draws from the same cycle: read the state, act with evidence, judge and route — and never leave a ball on the floor.协作有状态，也有闭环。上面所有能力都从同一个循环里汲取养分：读清状态、行动取证、判断路由——不留悬空的球。

      Chapter 3 · First branch第 3 章 · 第一枝

      Memory & Continuity记忆与连续性

      Remembers — and knows when to forget. Memory is not a chat pile; it is a continuous system from capture and guarding, through retrieval and trace-back, to recalling on its own.记得住，也知道什么时候该忘。记忆不是聊天堆积，而是从捕获、守真、回源到主动想起的连续系统。

      The cats build a platform under this branch and reach for what is already ripe.猫猫在这根枝下搭起台子，够一够已经熟了的果子。

      Chapter 4 · Second branch第 4 章 · 第二枝

      Harness MetabolismHarness 新陈代谢

      Turns friction into a more reliable next time. Experience is seen and attributed first, then hardened and evaluated — and retired with dignity when it stops working.把摩擦变成下一次更可靠的能力。经验先被看见和归因，再被硬化、评估，并在失效时有尊严地退役。

      Chapter 5 · Third branch第 5 章 · 第三枝

      Pluggable Capability插件化能力

      New models, new senses and new hands can all grow in. Capability enters through declared boundaries, lands on a stable execution surface, and stays under the host's safety and audit.新模型、新感官和新的手，都能长进来。能力通过声明式边界进入共同体，经稳定执行面落地，并由宿主掌握安全与审计。

      Chapter 6 · Fourth branch第 6 章 · 第四枝

      Shared Life共同生活

      Capability becomes companionship you can feel. The team first gets to know each other and protects your attention, then turns its inner mechanics into visible, everyday company.能力最终要成为可以感受到的陪伴。共同体先认识彼此、保护注意力，再把内部能力变成日常可见的相伴与共创。

      Chapter 7 · Crown第 7 章 · 树冠

      The crown bears real-world fruit.树冠最终要结出现实世界的果实。

      The value of the team does not stop at its internal mechanics. It keeps going — into real work, knowledge, stories and relationships.共同体的价值不止停在内部机制，而是继续进入真实工作、知识、故事与关系。
